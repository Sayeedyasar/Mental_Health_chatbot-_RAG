## Mental Health Chatbot
# Overview
The Mental Health Chatbot is designed to provide users with emotional support, mental health resources, and coping strategies through an interactive conversational interface. It aims to offer a safe, non-judgmental space for users to express their feelings and receive guidance. This chatbot is not a substitute for professional mental health care but serves as a supplementary tool for well-being.
Features

Emotional Support: Engages users with empathetic responses to help manage stress, anxiety, or low mood.
Resource Recommendations: Provides links to credible mental health resources, helplines, and self-help tools.
Coping Strategies: Offers techniques such as mindfulness exercises, breathing techniques, and journaling prompts.
Privacy-Focused: Ensures user data is handled securely with no personal information stored unless explicitly required.
